I'm interested in voice agents, latency, telephony and pretty much anything that has to happen in real time. Since February 2025 I've been the tech lead on FlowForge, the voice agent platform at Realbotics, on contract.

FlowForge Feb 2025 to now

We built it from zero with a team of four, and I built basically all of app.realbotics.ai. It does around 20k calls a day now, for LegalZoom, 1‑800Accountant and a couple of others. We run our own LiveKit (server, 4 SIP nodes, egress, TURN) with Twilio and customer SIP trunks on the phone side.

A lot of the work is figuring out where a call is losing time. Some of what I fixed:

  • Handing a caller from one agent to another mid call used to take 5.5 seconds, with the caller on the line the whole time. It's 31ms now.
  • A worker used to hold about 6 calls at once, now it's about 31. That took four bottlenecks, one after the other, and cold start p95 went from 4.6s to 1.7s on the way.
  • Under campaign load SIP calls would go from 20% failing to 100% failing in 8 minutes. Found why and fixed it.
  • Voicemail detection was treating 3.7% of real people as voicemail. Tuned it against 13k+ real calls and it's at 1%.
  • Prompt cache hit rate went from 68% to 98%. On a phone call that matters, a miss is 2.1s to first token and a hit is 0.6s.
  • We had issues with AWS, so I moved production to Hetzner and had it running there in under 24 hours. Deploys are blue/green and drain live calls before anything goes down.
  • Load tested at 200 concurrent calls, 402 of 402 completed.
  • Also a test bench for agents, with simulated callers that have personas, each tool mocked, and LLM judges.
