FlowForge Feb 2025 to now

I've been technical lead at Realbotics (on contract) since Feb 2025. I built FlowForge, their voice agent platform, from zero, and I lead its team of four. It handles around 100k calls a day for LegalZoom, 1‑800Accountant and others, and I built essentially all of app.realbotics.ai. We run all of LiveKit ourselves (server, SIP, egress, TURN).

Most recently I built recruitment screener agents on it in 8 languages (English, Hindi, Hinglish, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi, Bengali), and each one passes live-call checks with native-speaker audio.

A lot of the work is figuring out where a call is losing time. Some of what I did there:

  • When we had issues with AWS, I moved production to Hetzner and had it running there in under 24 hours. Deploys are blue/green and drain live calls before anything goes down.
  • Handing a caller from one agent to another mid call used to take 5.5 seconds, with the caller on the line the whole time. It's 31ms now.
  • A worker used to hold about 6 calls at once, now it's about 31. That took four bottlenecks, one after the other, and cold start p95 went from 4.6s to 1.7s on the way.
  • Under campaign load SIP calls would go from 20% failing to 100% failing in 8 minutes. Found why and fixed it.
  • Voicemail detection was treating 3.7% of real people as voicemail. Tuned it against 13k+ real calls and it's at 1%.
  • Prompt cache hit rate went from 68% to 98%. On a phone call that matters, a miss is 2.1s to first token and a hit is 0.6s.
  • Also a test bench for agents, with simulated callers that have personas, each tool mocked, and LLM judges.
